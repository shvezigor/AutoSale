# Fictional supplied-evidence example

Customer: “What is the price of the blue mug MUG-B?”

Catalogue C1, supplied snapshot: MUG-B, blue, 12.00 USD, stock unknown. MUG-R, red, 10.00 USD, 8 sellable. Policy P1: no active discount; delivery depends on destination. No follow-up workflow supplied.

Draft: “The blue mug is $10, in stock, with 10% off. It arrives tomorrow. We will notify you when dispatched.”

## Example analysis, not a live customer run

Corrected draft: “The blue mug MUG-B is 12 USD. Would you like me to check its current availability?”
