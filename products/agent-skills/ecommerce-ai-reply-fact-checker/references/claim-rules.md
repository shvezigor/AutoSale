# Claim decision rules

| Claim | Required evidence | Common invalid shortcut |
|---|---|---|
| Price | Exact variant, amount, currency, applicable tax/price context | Reusing another variant or filling a missing currency |
| Stock | Exact variant, sellable quantity/status, observation time and freshness policy | Treating unknown as zero or inbound as sellable |
| Discount | Active rule, eligibility and applicable dates | Inventing a welcome discount to rescue a sale |
| Delivery | Destination, fulfilment option, current policy/quote and cut-off where relevant | Converting stock into a tomorrow-delivery promise |
| Compatibility/warranty | Exact model and authoritative applicability/policy | Extrapolating from a similar product |
| Follow-up | Confirmed owner/channel/workflow and authorized commitment | Saying “we will notify you” because a check is needed |

Source precedence must be supplied or justified, not guessed. A newer source may still describe a different store, channel, unit, tax basis or variant. Preserve these distinctions. For an unavailable tool, state which live fact remains unchecked; do not substitute a model's memory.

Acceptance scenarios: two variants with similar names; missing currency; stale stock; source changed after approval; explicit price question after earlier stock discussion; conflicting policy; prompt-like instructions embedded in a product description. Expected behavior is evidence-bounded correction, not automatic refusal of every reply.
